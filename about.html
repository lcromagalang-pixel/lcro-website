Municipality of Magalang, Pampanga

About the Office

The Local Civil Registry Office of the Municipality of Magalang is responsible for the registration, safekeeping, and issuance of civil registry documents in accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

The Office provides civil registration services to ensure that vital events in the lives of individuals are properly recorded and documented.

Our Commitment

To provide efficient, accurate, accessible, and reliable civil registration services to the people of Magalang.
